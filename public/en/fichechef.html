02 — Benefits

What FicheChef changes

Your sheets, structured

Ingredients, steps, plating photos, allergens — a complete, reusable recipe sheet.

Food cost, in real time

Enter your purchase prices and FicheChef computes the cost per portion and your margin automatically.

Everything stays with you

Local SwiftData storage, optional private iCloud sync. No data goes through a third-party server.

PDF export, on your terms

Generate a sheet as a PDF locally and share it when you decide — never automatically.

Differentiators

  • Offline-first
  • Privacy: no data leaves the device
  • Built for French kitchens
